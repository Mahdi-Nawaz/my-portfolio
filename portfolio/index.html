Project

Featured · Internship project

Customer Churn Prediction

An end-to-end analytics project on the IBM Telco dataset (7,043 customers, 21 attributes). It finds why customers leave, predicts who is at risk, and presents the results through a web app and a dashboard.

What I did

  • Cleaned the data: fixed the text-typed TotalCharges column and 11 blank values that belonged to brand-new customers.
  • Analysed churn drivers. Month-to-month contracts churn at 42.7% against 2.8% on two-year contracts, and 55.5% of churners left in their first year.
  • Compared Logistic Regression, Random Forest and XGBoost on a stratified 80/20 split. Logistic Regression reached ROC-AUC 0.842 and 81% accuracy; XGBoost caught the most churners (70% recall).
  • Explained the model with SHAP and built a Streamlit app and a Power BI dashboard for revenue at risk.
