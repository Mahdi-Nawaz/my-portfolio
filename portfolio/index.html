Internship Project · Recimo Tech ML & BI

Customer Churn Prediction & Revenue Intelligence

An end-to-end predictive machine learning solution on 7,043 customer accounts (IBM Telco dataset) pinpointing why customers churn, isolating high-risk cohorts, and translating predictions into retention strategies via a live prediction app and Power BI dashboard.

Quantitative Results & Key Findings

  • Data Wrangling: Cleaned the text-typed TotalCharges column and properly imputed 11 blank values belonging to newly onboarded customers.
  • Churn Disparities: Discovered that month-to-month contracts suffer a 42.7% churn rate compared to only 2.8% for two-year contracts, and 55.5% of all churners leave within year one.
  • Model Benchmarking: Trained and tested Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost on a stratified 80/20 split. Logistic Regression delivered ROC-AUC 0.842 and 81% accuracy; XGBoost captured 70% recall on churners.
  • Explainable AI (XAI): Interpreted feature importance using SHAP values (tenure, contract type, and monthly charges driving risk).
  • Business Deliverables: Built a Streamlit predictive inference app and an executive Power BI dashboard calculating revenue-at-risk.
